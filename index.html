Our story

Built for the ten-minute walk between weather systems.

Solace Apparel started in 2016 in a shared studio space in the Los Angeles Arts District, founded by two former outdoor-gear pattern cutters who were tired of choosing between clothes that looked good and clothes that worked. The idea was simple: streetwear silhouettes, built with the material logic of technical outerwear.

Every Solace piece starts with a question — what is this garment actually for? A jacket has to survive a sudden downpour on the walk to the train. A pant has to bend at the knee on a bike and still look right at a desk. We design backward from that use case, then strip away anything that doesn’t earn its place. The result is a wardrobe that reads as minimal because it is minimal — every seam, pocket, and zipper pull is there on purpose.

01

Sustainability

70% of our fabric library is recycled or deadstock, and every shell fabric is bluesign®-approved. We publish our supply chain twice a year.

02

Quality Materials

Japanese ripstop nylon, YKK zippers, and cotton milled in Portugal. We test every seam to 20,000 flex cycles before it ships.

03

Limited Drops

We run four numbered drops a year in small batches. When a colourway sells out, it’s gone — we don’t restock for the sake of it.
